SUBJECT: COURT DIRECTIVE ON POLICE COMPLAINTS AUTHORITY

(Summary of the question and the Government's answer, paraphrased for a practice corpus.
Source: Parliament Digital Library, eparlib.nic.in, document 117961.)


QUESTION (wording reconstructed from the structure of the answer)

The Member asked the Minister of Home Affairs:
(a) whether the Supreme Court had directed the setting up of Police Complaints Authorities;
(b) the steps taken by the Government and the States to implement that direction; and
(c) the reasons for any delay.


ANSWER

(a) Yes. By its judgment dated 22 September 2006 in Writ Petition (Civil) No. 310 of 1996, Prakash Singh and Others vs Union of India and Others, the Supreme Court directed the State Governments and the Central Government to set up Police Complaints Authorities at the State and district levels to look into complaints against police officers.

(b) The judgment was circulated to all State Governments. In 2008 the Supreme Court constituted a Monitoring Committee headed by Justice K.T. Thomas to examine the affidavits filed by the States on compliance. The Committee's report was circulated on 4 October 2010. According to that report, 9 States had set up Police Complaints Authorities through executive instructions, and 12 States had done so through new or amended Police Acts. For the Union Territories, the Ministry of Home Affairs issued orders on 3 March 2010. The Supreme Court is still examining the implementation of its directions.

(c) "Police" is a State subject under the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution of India. Setting up Police Complaints Authorities therefore falls within the purview of the State Governments, and the matter is already under the consideration of the Supreme Court.


KEY FACTS
- Judgment date: 22 September 2006
- Case: Prakash Singh and Others vs Union of India, WP (C) 310/1996
- Monitoring Committee: Justice K.T. Thomas (constituted 2008), report circulated 4 October 2010
- States using executive instructions: 9
- States using Police Acts: 12
- MHA orders for Union Territories: 3 March 2010
- Constitutional position: Police is a State subject (Seventh Schedule)
